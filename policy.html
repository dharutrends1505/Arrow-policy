Our third-party services may automatically process certain technical information, including:

We use Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes, errors, and technical problems.

Crashlytics may process information such as:

  • Crash logs

3. Firebase Services

Arrow Away 3D: Escape Puzzle uses certain Firebase services provided by Google.

These may include:

  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase App Check

Firebase may process technical information necessary to provide these services, protect backend resources, deliver messages, diagnose crashes, and operate enabled Firebase functionality.
